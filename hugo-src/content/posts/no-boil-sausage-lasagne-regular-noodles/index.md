## No-Boil Sausage Lasagne (with Regular Noodles)

This uses **regular lasagne noodles**, not oven-ready noodles. Do not boil them first. They soften in the sauce while the lasagne bakes.

### Ingredients

- 8 oz regular lasagne noodles (1/2 box), uncooked, not oven-ready
- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (15 oz) container ricotta cheese
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 2 large jars (about 50 oz) pasta sauce, your favorite kind
- Red pepper flakes to taste

### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, combine ricotta, 1 cup mozzarella, Parmesan, and eggs. Mix well.
3. Cook the onion in a large pan over medium heat until soft, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. Make a space in the center of the pan. Add the sausage and let it brown on one side without stirring.
6. Flip the sausage, break it up, and keep cooking until browned.
7. Stir in the pasta sauce and red pepper flakes.
8. Spread 1 cup of the sauce mixture in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
9. Add a layer of regular uncooked lasagne noodles, then half of the ricotta mixture, then more sauce.
10. Repeat the layering with the remaining noodles, ricotta mixture, and sauce.
11. Top with the remaining 1 cup mozzarella.
12. Cover with foil and bake for 55 minutes.
13. Uncover and bake for 5 more minutes, until the cheese is bubbly.
14. Let rest for 15 minutes before cutting.

### Note on the noodles

Use regular lasagne noodles straight from the box. Do not boil them, and do not use oven-ready noodles.

---

This is one of those recipes that just works. Browning the sausage before adding the sauce gives it a little more richness. No boiling and using jarred sauce keeps it easy enough for a weeknight or a crowd.
